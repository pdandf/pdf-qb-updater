Connect or Reconnect QuickBooks

PD&F QuickBooks Data Updater is an internal-use Windows application for Plastic Displays & Fixtures Co., Inc. It retrieves authorized QuickBooks Online records to support company order tracking and reporting.

Connection and reconnection are initiated from the private Windows updater on an authorized company computer, not from this website.

  1. Open the PD&F QuickBooks Data Updater on the authorized Windows computer.
  2. Choose the application's Connect or Reconnect option (once implemented).
  3. Follow the Intuit authorization prompts and select the intended QuickBooks Online company.
  4. Return to the local application to confirm connection status.

Important: This webpage does not sign in to QuickBooks, collect passwords, perform OAuth authorization, or connect the application automatically. The connection function is being implemented in the private Windows updater.

For disconnection instructions, see Disconnect QuickBooks.
