Disconnect QuickBooks

PD&F QuickBooks Data Updater is an internal-use Windows application for Plastic Displays & Fixtures Co., Inc.

An authorized QuickBooks administrator can revoke the application's access directly in QuickBooks Online:

  1. Sign in to QuickBooks Online as an administrator.
  2. Open Integrations (sometimes called My integrations) and choose Manage integrations.
  3. Find PDF Order Data Updater or the corresponding application entry.
  4. Open its menu, choose Disconnect, and confirm. Menu labels may vary.

Disconnecting prevents future authorized API access. It does not automatically delete previously exported spreadsheets or PDFs stored by your company in OneDrive. Those files must be managed separately. If local OAuth credentials have been stored, remove them through the updater's connection management workflow as well.

To learn how the private Windows application reconnects, see Connect or Reconnect QuickBooks.

See Intuit's disconnection instructions for current QuickBooks menu details.
