Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes the handling of information by PD&F QB Data Updater (the “Application”), an internal tool used by Plastic Displays & Fixtures Co., Inc. (the “Company”).

1. Purpose and authorized users

The Application is for authorized Company personnel only. It is not a consumer service or publicly available accounting platform.

2. Information accessed

With Company authorization, the Application may retrieve QuickBooks Online purchase orders, invoice records, vendor bills, related customer and vendor references, transaction identifiers, amounts, dates, balances, and purchase-order PDFs. This information may contain business contact and transaction information.

3. Use of information

Retrieved information is used to maintain internal order-tracking spreadsheets, calculate or review accounting-related status information, and archive purchase-order PDFs. The Application is designed to perform read operations against QuickBooks and not to create, edit, or delete QuickBooks transactions.

4. Storage and access

Outputs may be saved to Company-controlled local computer folders and Microsoft OneDrive for Business. Application configuration and authorization credentials are maintained separately from public policy pages and accounting workbooks, with access intended to be limited to authorized Company users. The Company may use backups of its internal files.

5. Third-party services

The Application uses Intuit QuickBooks Online to retrieve authorized records and may use Microsoft OneDrive for Business to store or synchronize exported files. Those providers handle information under their respective terms and policies.

6. Sharing and sale of information

The Company does not use this Application to sell QuickBooks accounting information or disclose it for advertising. Information may be accessed by authorized Company personnel and by the service providers necessary to operate QuickBooks and Company file storage.

7. Retention and security

Records and exported files are retained according to Company operational, accounting, and legal needs. The Company uses access controls and reasonable safeguards appropriate to an internal-use tool. No method of storage or transfer is completely risk-free.

8. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated if the Application’s functionality or data handling changes. Questions may be directed to Plastic Displays & Fixtures Co., Inc. through its website at plasticdf.com.
